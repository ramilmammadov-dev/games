Dock Shift Support

Dock Shift is a free block jam puzzle set in a container port. Slide shipping containers across the quay into the truck bay of their own color and width before the shift clock runs out.

How to play

  • Drag a container to slide it across the grid. Flick it to make it glide until something stops it.
  • Push it into the truck bay that matches its color and is wide enough. A truck backs in, loads it and drives away.
  • A container stacked on another one must leave first. The box under it stays locked until then.
  • Ship cargo boards in the order shown on the manifest slots next to the ship.
  • Crane Hop lifts one box over the others, +15 SEC adds time and Hammer smashes one box. Tap a booster, then use it on the quay.
  • Clear every container before the clock ends to earn stars and coins. Each cleared port adds a ship to your Fleet.
