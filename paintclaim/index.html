Paint Claim Support

Paint Claim is a free territory battle game. Steer your blob, draw loops outside your land and claim everything inside. Cut rival trails to knock them out and own the biggest part of the map.

How to play

  • Swipe to steer your blob.
  • Leave your land to draw a trail, then come back to claim the loop.
  • If a rival touches your trail you are out, so keep loops short when rivals are near.
