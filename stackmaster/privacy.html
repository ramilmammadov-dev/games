Stack Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Stack Master - Block Tower is a game made by Ramil Mammadov. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or past activity. AdMob may process technical information such as IP address, device model, general location and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Stack Master is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

All progress is on your device. Delete the app to remove it.
