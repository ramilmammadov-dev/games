Orbit Siege Support

Orbit Siege is a free space conquest game. Swipe from your planet to send a stream of ships, trade shots with the rival fleet and capture every enemy planet in 150 sectors across 6 galaxies.

How to play

  • Swipe from one of your planets to any other planet to launch half of its fleet.
  • Drag through several of your planets and end on the target to send one combined wave.
  • Ships trade one for one with defenders. A planet changes owner when its count passes zero.
  • Shipyards build ships twice as fast, Bastions halve incoming damage and Relays launch faster fleets.
  • Black holes bend and eat ships, and asteroid belts slow them down.
  • After each sector pick one of three perks for the rest of your run.
