Final Kick Support

Final Kick is a free free kick football game. Swipe to shoot, bend your swipe to curl the ball around the wall and beat the keeper under the floodlights.

How to play

  • Swipe up toward the goal to shoot. A faster swipe kicks harder.
  • Bend your swipe to curl the ball around the wall.
  • Score 3 of 5 kicks to win the match. Top corners count double.
