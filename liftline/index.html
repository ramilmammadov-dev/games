Lift Line Support

Lift Line is a free ski resort crowd puzzle. Tap skiers out of the corral and into the lift cabin that matches their color.

How to play

  • Tap a skier who has a clear way to the gate. They walk to the lift.
  • If the waiting cabin has their color they hop in. If not, they sit on a bench.
  • Full cabins climb away and the next color swings in. If the benches are full, the level is over.
  • Roped groups board together, ski patrol fits any cabin, and skiers cannot turn on ice.
