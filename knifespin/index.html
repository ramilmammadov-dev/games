Knife Spin Support

Knife Spin is a free one tap knife throwing game. Tap to throw your knife into the spinning log and never hit a blade that is already stuck.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to throw a knife.
  • Never hit a knife that is already in the target.
  • Slice apples on the rim for coins. Stick every knife to break the target.
  • Every 5th stage is a boss target that drops a rare knife.
