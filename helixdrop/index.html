Helix Drop Support

Helix Drop is a free one finger arcade game. Twist the neon tower, drop the ball through the gaps and smash through rings in FEVER mode.

How to play

  • Drag left or right to twist the tower.
  • Drop the ball through the gaps and avoid the red parts.
  • Fall through 3 rings in a row to catch FEVER.
