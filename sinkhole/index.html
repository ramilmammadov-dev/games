Sinkhole Support

Sinkhole is a free one finger arcade game about a hole that swallows a whole city. Drag the hole through the streets, grow bigger with every bite and out eat the rival hole.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to move the sinkhole.
  • Anything smaller than the hole falls in and makes it bigger.
  • Eat as much of the city as you can before the clock runs out. From city 3 a rival hole digs too.
