Bank Shot Support

Bank Shot is a free pool puzzle game. Drag back to aim, pot the trick shot and earn up to three stars on every table.

How to play

  • Drag back from anywhere to aim. The dotted ghost ball line shows the first contact.
  • Pull farther for more power and let go to shoot.
  • Hit the goal on the banner: pot balls, bank them off cushions or pot them in order. Tap the spin ball for follow, draw or side spin.
