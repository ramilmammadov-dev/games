Blast Engineer Support

Blast Engineer is a free demolition physics game. Place charges on real looking buildings, hit detonate and watch them come down inside the target zone.

How to play

  • Drag charges onto the glowing points of the building.
  • Press DETONATE and watch the structure fall.
  • Keep the debris inside the target zone and use as few charges as you can for an A+ report.
