Calm Lake Support

Calm Lake is a free relaxing fishing game. Cast from an old wooden dock, hook the bite and reel in 45 kinds of fish from sunset to midday.

How to play

  • Hold to charge your cast and let go at the top of the meter for a perfect, long throw.
  • When the bobber dips, tap at once to hook the fish.
  • Hold to reel and let go to ease. Keep the needle inside the green zone or the line snaps.
  • Open the Fish Book from the main menu to see your records and upgrade your rod, reel and line.
