Sudoku Clean Support

Sudoku Clean is a free, calm and classic Sudoku game with 4 difficulty levels, smart notes and a new Daily Puzzle every day.

How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row, column and 3x3 box has 1 to 9 once.
  • Tap a cell, then tap a number. Turn on Notes for pencil marks.
  • 3 mistakes end the game. Use Hint when you are stuck.
