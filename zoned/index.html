Zoned Support

Zoned is a free logic puzzle where you plan a town. Build exactly one house in every row, every column and every colored district. Houses may not touch, not even at the corners.

How to play

  • Tap a plot to build a house. A right house pops up and trees and fences grow on the plots it rules out.
  • A wrong house collapses and costs one of your 3 permits. Lose all 3 and the block starts again.
  • Hold a plot to mark it as a park. Tap the park to remove it.
  • Lakes cannot be built on. Landmarks are houses that are already built. Twin plots are one district in two parts that holds only one house.
  • Use HINT to see the next safe house, TREES to turn auto landscaping on or off, and PERMIT to get a lost permit back.
  • Finish a block to watch the roads draw themselves, the cars drive in and the windows light up at dusk.
