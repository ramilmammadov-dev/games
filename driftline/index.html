Drift Line Support

Drift Line is a free one touch drift game on an endless mountain road. Hold to drift, release to straighten out and keep the car on the road.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere to drift into the corner.
  • Release when the car lines up with the next stretch of road.
  • Finish a drift on the center line for a PERFECT. Miss the road and you go over the edge.
