Golf Isles Support

Golf Isles is a free mini golf game played on floating islands in the sky. Pull back from the ball, aim and sink every hole in as few strokes as you can.

How to play

  • Pull back from the ball to aim. Pull farther for a stronger putt.
  • Let go to putt. Par or better earns 3 stars.
  • Falling off the island or into water costs one stroke.
