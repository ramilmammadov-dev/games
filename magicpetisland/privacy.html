Magic Pet Island Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Magic Pet Island is a game made by Ramil Mammadov. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as IP address, device model, general location, ad interactions and, where allowed, the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. On iOS the advertising identifier is available to ads only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Magic Pet Island is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

All progress is on your device. Delete the app to remove it.
