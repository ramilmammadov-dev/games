Postcards Support

Postcards is a free, cozy jigsaw solitaire. Every picture is a tiny 3D town cut into cards. Swap the cards, lock the neighbors and watch the postcard come alive.

How to play

  • Drag a card onto another spot to swap them.
  • Cards that belong side by side snap together into a group.
  • Drag a locked group to move it as one piece.
  • Finish the picture to earn stars and a postmark for your album.
