Skyburst Support

Skyburst is a free arrow escape puzzle with fireworks. Every rocket flies straight ahead, the way its nose points. Tap the rockets with a clear path and clear the rooftop for a grand sky show.

How to play

  • Tap a rocket to launch it. It races along its fuse and bursts over the city.
  • If another rocket or a chimney is in the way, it bounces back and you lose one of 3 matches.
  • Hold a rocket to preview its path. Tied rockets launch together, so both paths must be clear.
  • Use HINT to find a free rocket and UNDO to get back your last lost match.
