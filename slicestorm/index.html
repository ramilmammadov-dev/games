Slice Storm Support

Slice Storm is a free fruit slicing arcade game with Classic, Arcade and Zen modes.

How to play

  • Swipe across the screen to slice fruit.
  • Slice 3 or more in one swipe for a combo.
  • Do not slice the bombs.
