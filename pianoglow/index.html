Piano Glow Support

Piano Glow is a free music tiles game. Tap the glowing tiles to play famous classical melodies.

How to play

  • Tap the tiles from the bottom up. Every tile plays the next note.
  • Hold the long tiles until they fill.
  • Do not miss a tile and do not tap empty lanes.
