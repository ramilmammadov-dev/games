Ludo Royale Support

Ludo Royale is a free, relaxing block puzzle. Drag shapes onto the board, clear rows and columns, and make your garden bloom.

How to play

  • Tap the dice on your turn, then tap a glowing token.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Land on rivals to send them home.
  • Bring all 4 tokens to the center to win.
