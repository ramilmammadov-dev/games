Potion Sort Support

Potion Sort is a free color sorting puzzle set in an alchemy lab. Pour the liquids until every flask holds one color, then collect the potions you brew.

How to play

  • Tap a flask, then tap another flask to pour into it.
  • You can pour only onto the same color or into an empty flask.
  • Fill a flask with one color to brew a potion. Dark smoky layers show their color when they reach the top.
  • Stuck? Use Undo, Restart or add one extra empty flask.
