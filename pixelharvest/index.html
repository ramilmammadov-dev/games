Pixel Harvest Support

Pixel Harvest is a free color sort puzzle on a farm. Every field is a pixel picture planted as real crops. Send your harvesters around the farm road and cut the picture away, color by color.

How to play

  • Tap the front harvester of a barn lane to send it onto the farm road.
  • On its lap it cuts the first crop of its own color in every row and column it faces.
  • When its number reaches 0 it drives to the silo. If it still has room after a lap, it parks in a bay. Tap a parked harvester to send it again.
  • Clear the whole picture before all the bays are full. Fill a tank in one lap for a Perfect Haul and double coins.
  • Stuck? Use the Sickle, Shuffle or Extra Bay boosters, or retry the field.
