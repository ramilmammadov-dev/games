Hill Rider Support

Hill Rider is a free physics driving game. Climb endless hills, flip in the air, grab fuel and coins and upgrade your car.

How to play

  • Hold GAS to drive and BRAKE to slow down or reverse.
  • In the air, GAS tilts the car back and BRAKE tilts it forward.
  • Grab fuel cans and keep the driver's head off the ground.
