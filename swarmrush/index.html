Swarm Rush Support

Swarm Rush is a free crowd battle game. Pour your ant swarm through dew drop gates, multiply it and flood the red colony to claim each territory.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere to pour ants out of your hill.
  • Drag left and right to steer the stream through the gates. Brown leaves with red numbers take ants away.
  • A big ant drops in every 10 seconds. Break the queen's chamber to win.
