Scrap Match Support

Scrap Match is a free 3D match puzzle set in a junkyard. A magnet crane pulls cars, wheels and cones out of a physics heap and drops them into a crusher row. Crush three of a kind into a scrap cube and build your scrap tower across 400 yards.

How to play

  • Tap a car, wheel, cone or crate in the heap. The crane pulls it into one of 7 crusher slots.
  • Three of the same model in the row are crushed into a scrap cube. If all slots fill up, the yard is lost.
  • Chained cars come out together and take two slots. Rusty junk needs two taps.
  • Clear the whole yard before the clock runs out. Boosters: Magnet Sweep, Shake and Extra Slot.
