Mahjong Bloom Support

Mahjong Bloom is a free, calm mahjong solitaire game. Match pairs of free tiles, clear the board and solve a new Daily Puzzle every day.

How to play

  • Tap two matching tiles to clear them.
  • A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is open.
  • Use Hint, Undo or Shuffle if you get stuck.
