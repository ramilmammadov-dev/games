Mahjong Bloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Mahjong Bloom is a free game made by Ramil Mammadov. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress (scores, coins, levels, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

Mahjong Bloom shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model and ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. On iOS you are asked through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether ads may use your advertising identifier. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are less personalized. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form, and can change their choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Choices in the game.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

More Games list

The More Games screen downloads a small public list of our other games and their icons. No personal data is sent.

Children

Mahjong Bloom is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

All progress is on your device. Use Settings > Reset Progress in the game, or delete the app, to remove it.
