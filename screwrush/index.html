Screw Rush Support

Screw Rush is a free nuts and bolts puzzle. Unscrew colorful screws, fill the tool boxes and clear every machine.

How to play

  • Tap a screw to unscrew it. It flies to the tool box of the same color.
  • If no box matches, it waits in the tray. Do not let the tray fill up.
  • Screws under another plate are blocked.
