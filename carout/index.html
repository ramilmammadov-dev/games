Car Out Support

Car Out is a free parking puzzle. Tap a car to drive it out of the lot, find the right order and clear every level.

How to play

  • Tap a car to drive it out.
  • A car can only leave if nothing blocks its way, forward or backward.
  • Clear the lot with no bumps for 3 stars.
