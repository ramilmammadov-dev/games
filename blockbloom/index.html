Block Bloom Support

Block Bloom is a free, relaxing block puzzle. Drag shapes onto the board, clear rows and columns, and make your garden bloom.

How to play

  • Drag one of the three shapes onto the 8x8 board.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  • Fill the Bloom meter to clear every block of the most common color.
