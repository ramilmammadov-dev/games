Singularity Support

Singularity: Chain Reaction is a strategy game of orbs and chain reactions. Fill cells with your orbs, push them past their limit and watch them burst into the neighbors, taking them over in long chain reactions.

How to play

  • Tap an empty cell or one of your own cells to add an orb.
  • A cell bursts when it holds as many orbs as it has neighbors, sending one orb to each neighbor and turning it to your color.
  • Bursts can trigger more bursts. Take every orb on the board to win.
  • Beat the AI from Beginner to Master and unlock bigger grids and themes as you level up.

Singularity Pro

Pro (monthly or yearly) unlocks the Expert and Master opponents at once, every grid size and theme, and removes ads. Free players can play a Pro match by watching a short ad. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Restore Purchases is in the game's Settings.
