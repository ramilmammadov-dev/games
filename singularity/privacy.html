Singularity Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Singularity: Chain Reaction is a game made by Ramil Mammadov. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress (levels, statistics, achievements, daily rewards, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

Free players see ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model, general location and ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. On iOS you are asked through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether ads may use your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Singularity Pro subscriptions

Singularity Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is handled by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your payment details. To unlock Pro on your device we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and a random app user ID that is not linked to your identity. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.

More Games list

The More Games screen downloads a small public list of our other games and their icons. No personal data is sent.

Children

Singularity is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

All progress is on your device. Delete the app to remove it. Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing.
