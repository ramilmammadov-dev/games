Solitaire Voyage Support

Solitaire Voyage is a free classic Klondike solitaire game with big clear cards, Draw 1 and Draw 3 modes and a Daily Deal.

How to play

  • Build four foundations from Ace to King, one per suit.
  • Stack cards on the columns downwards in alternating colors.
  • Tap a card to move it, tap the deck to draw. Undo and Hint any time.
