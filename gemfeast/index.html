Gem Feast Support

Gem Feast is a free merge puzzle game with three bonus gem games: Gem Crush, Gem Sort and Gem 2048.

How to play

  • Drag and release to drop items into the jar. Two identical items merge into the next one.
  • At the Prism Star, choose the Cake or Gem path.
  • Play Classic, Rush (90 seconds) or Zen (no game over).
