Royal Tiles Support

Royal Tiles is a free match 3 puzzle with royal treasures and hundreds of levels.

How to play

  • Swipe a treasure into its neighbor to swap them.
  • Match 3 or more to collect them. Bigger matches make special tiles.
  • Reach the level goal before you run out of moves.
