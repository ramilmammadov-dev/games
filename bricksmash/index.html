Brick Smash Support

Brick Smash is a free neon brick breaker. Drag to aim, release to fire a stream of balls, and shatter glass bricks before they reach the bottom.

How to play

  • Drag to aim. The dotted line shows the path.
  • Release to fire all your balls. Every hit lowers a brick's number.
  • Catch +1 rings for more balls. Use bombs, lasers and split orbs.
  • Bricks move down every turn. Do not let them reach the bottom line.
