Rainbow Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Rainbow Rescue is a game made by Ramil Mammadov. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for a name, email or any account. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

In-app purchase

Levels 1 to 3 are free. Grown-ups can buy the optional Full Game with a one-time purchase. The purchase screen is behind a grown-up check. Payment is handled by Apple, and we never see payment details.

To confirm the purchase and restore it on other devices, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, the purchase record from Apple and basic technical data such as IP address, device model, operating system and app version. This data is used only to deliver the purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not linked to a name or email.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Rainbow Rescue is made for young children. We do not ask children for any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads and no links out of the app, and only a grown-up can open the purchase screen.

Deleting your data

All progress is on your device. Delete the app to remove it. To ask for purchase records to be deleted, email us.
