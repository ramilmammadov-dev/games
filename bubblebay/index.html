Bubble Bay Support

Bubble Bay is a free bubble shooter. Aim, bounce off the walls and match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.

How to play

  • Drag to aim, release to shoot.
  • Match 3 or more of the same color. Hanging bubbles fall for bonus points.
  • Fill the POWER meter for a Bomb or a Rainbow bubble.
