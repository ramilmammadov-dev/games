Garage Boss Support

Garage Boss is a free idle arcade tycoon about a roadside car shop. Run, carry tires, oil and paint to the waiting cars, collect the cash and grow your shop.

How to play

  • Drag your finger to run.
  • Walk into a rack to grab tires, oil cans or paint, then walk into a bay to fix the waiting car.
  • Run over the cash and stand on glowing pads to unlock stations, hire mechanics and buy upgrades.
  • Rush Hour doubles the pay for a short time every few minutes.
